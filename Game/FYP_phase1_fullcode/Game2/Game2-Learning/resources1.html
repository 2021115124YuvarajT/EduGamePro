Types of Waste

Organic Waste

Organic waste is anything that comes from plants or animals. This can be things like leftover food, fruit peels, or leaves. We can turn this into compost, which helps plants grow better.

Recyclable Waste

Recyclable waste includes things like paper, plastic bottles, and metal cans. We can reuse these materials to make new things, which helps keep our planet clean and healthy.

Toxic Waste

Hazardous waste is dangerous, like old batteries, paint, or chemicals. These things need special care so they don't hurt people or the environment.

Soiled Waste

Items contaminated with body fluids, like used tissues, diapers, or medical waste. These often require incineration or special disposal methods.These are called Soiled wastes.

E-Waste

Discarded electronic devices like computers, phones, and appliances. E-waste contains materials that can be harmful and valuable, so proper recycling is necessary.
