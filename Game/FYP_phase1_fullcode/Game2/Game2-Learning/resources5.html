Float and Sink

Float

Materials that have a density lower than the liquid will rise to the surface. For example, in recycling, lighter plastics may float, allowing them to be separated from heavier materials.
Most types of wood, like pine or oak, are less dense than water and float.
Ice is less dense than liquid water, so ice cubes float.

Sink

Materials with a higher density than the liquid will sink to the bottom. This is useful for separating denser materials like glass or metals from lighter ones.
Metals like iron, steel, and copper sink in water because they are much denser.
Most rocks, like granite or marble, have a higher density than water and sink.
