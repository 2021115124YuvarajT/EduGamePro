Types of Fibres

Natural Fibres

Natural fibres come from plants, animals, or minerals. Examples include cotton, wool, and silk. These fibres are biodegradable and eco-friendly.

Synthetic Fibres

Synthetic fibres are man-made and produced from chemical substances. Examples include nylon, polyester, and acrylic. These fibres are strong and durable, but not biodegradable.
